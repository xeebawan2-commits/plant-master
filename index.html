Husky Machines 120,000 Cycle Preventive Maintenance & Mold Overhaul Schedule

Comprehensive major maintenance (`Machines marked RED have reached 120,000 cycles`)

120,000 Cycle Complete Machine & Mold Overhaul Mandatory Tasks

🛠️ Mold Maintenance & Cleaning Protocol
  • 1. Mold Cores Maintenance & Cleaning: Ultrasonic chemical descaling to remove scale, mineral deposits, and PET oligomer build-up from internal core cooling channels.
  • 2. Neck Rings Inspection & Cleaning: Disassemble neck ring tapers, clean parting lines with brass scrapers, verify wear tolerances, and check taper alignment.
  • 3. Mold Cavities Polishing: Inspect preform optical clarity, remove vent block deposits, and hand-polish cavity tapers with diamond compound.
  • 4. Cool-Pick Tubes Inspection & Cleaning: Clean post-mold water cooling tubes, verify vacuum pin actuation, and check water flow rates on each tube.
  • 5. Robot Take-Out Tubes Verification: Inspect robot vacuum suction cups for wear/cracks, clean vacuum venturis, and calibrate robot Z-axis alignment.
⚙️ Machine Clamp, Slides & Bearings Greasing
  • 6. Slides & Linear Bearings Greasing: Purge old grease from clamp moving platen linear guide rails, tie-bar bushings, and ejector slides using high-temperature synthetic grease.
  • 7. Clamp Cylinder High-Pressure Seals: Inspect clamp cylinder lock rings and high-pressure seals; verify tie-bar stretch tonnage across all 4 bars with electronic dial gauges.
  • 8. Shooting Pot & Hydraulic Filtration: Take hydraulic oil sample for ISO cleanliness testing, replace 10-micron return line filters, and check check-valve ball wear.
  • 9. Accumulator & Electrical Sign-off: Check nitrogen accumulator pre-charge bar pressure, retighten cabinet contactors, and perform safety gate interlock tests.
